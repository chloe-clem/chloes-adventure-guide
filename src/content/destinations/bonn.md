My friends and I stayed in Bonn not to see or do anything specific, but to escape the hustle and bustle of Cologne and get a feel for a quieter side of Germany.

I was surprised by how big Bonn still felt, and there were definitely a few moments near the train station that put us on edge. But we were staying in a residential area further outside the center, and once we got there it felt like a genuine "staycation." We hadn't come with a tourist mindset, more just to get a sense of the area and spend quality time together.

The real highlights were the castles. They were grand, fairy-tale kind of beautiful and surrounded by rich greenery. We also were surprised by all the history packed into the area. Beethoven grew up here. Lord Byron wrote a poem about Drachenburg Castle, the one we actually visited. And maybe my favorite random fact of the whole trip: Haribo was invented in Bonn, so of course we tracked down the store and stocked up on candy!
