Salzburg is an iconic place to visit because of its presence in "The Sound of Music". However, this is not why I went.

At the time, I had a friend studying here, so I made the trip out for Christmas as I was living only a short (kind of) bus ride away. It did feel a bit touristy seeing "The Sound of Music" tours constantly filling the streets, but I ended up having a great time. 

I loved walking along the river and through big, open fields where you'd see horses pass you on the left and iconic buildings from "The Sound of Music" to the right. 

The Christmas market was fairly small but I enjoyed walking around with a warm glass of glühwein in one hand, a tasty sandwich in the other, and admiring the twinkling lights and unique souvenirs.

The highlight, and the reason I'd go back, was how close the city was to outstanding nature. Even from the city you could see incredible mountain ranges, and it just got better the closer you got. For two days in a row, we took the bus towards the German border and spent the entire day hiking through snowy trails and frozen lakes. I remember feeling so at peace and in awe of the rugged landscapes. Those are two days I will always remember so clearly.
