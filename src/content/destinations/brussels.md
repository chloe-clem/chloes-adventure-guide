In all honesty, most of my trip planning comes from me pulling up Apple Maps and scrolling to a random location I think "looks" like it could be cool. I commit and figure out the rest later. This is exactly what I did with my Brussels trip. I wanted to explore more cities that were relatively close to the Netherlands, so this just felt like the right choice.

Unfortunately, a couple days out from my trip as I started telling people where I was going, their terrified reactions immediately made me rethink my uneducated decision. 

Needless to say, I arrived with very low expectations and a slight fear for my life. In good news, I was pleasantly surprised when I arrived.

Would I say it's my favorite city? Probably not. But the waffles made the trip worthwhile, especially the Brussels-style waffle (compared to the also popular Liège waffle). Strolling through the city and looking at the beautiful, gilded architecture and historic buildings made for a great day. I also enjoyed the fall colors in the park and visiting the cathedral.

The only time I felt really unsafe was leaving the train station late at night to track down my Flixbus on some random corner street. 

In summary, I'd say Brussels is worth a day stop if you're already passing by. Why not check off another big European city from your list? And you might have a better experience than me if you appreciate its rich political history as a capital city.
