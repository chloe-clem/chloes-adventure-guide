Sorrento was a cute little coastal town that made for a very nice stay and home base for visiting Pompeii. It's not a bustling place by any means, but there are still people out and about at night and lots of yummy food places to choose from. I also felt extremely safe here going out at night and exploring with my friends.

I was only here for a short time, but I thoroughly enjoyed the slower pace. It was very walkable as it's not a very big town, and the views along the coast are gorgeous. I was there in the fall time, but I imagine it's a great place to swim in the summer!
