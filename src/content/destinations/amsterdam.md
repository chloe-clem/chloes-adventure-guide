I think because Amsterdam is such a well known city, it can feel pretty busy and touristy at times. However, I think it is equally as charming. The Dutch architecture is so unique and it creates for a very aesthetic city. 

There are tons of museums to visit, fun food places to try (as long as you avoid the tourist traps), thrift stores if that's your thing, and fun activities. I highly recommend doing a sunset cruise on the canal, visiting the Van Gogh museum, and taking a tour of the Anne Frank house.

Do you need more than a day or 2 here? Probably not. Personally, I love the Netherlands more for its small towns. Each one feels like it comes straight out of a fairy tale and is much calmer than being right in the city. If you visit the Netherlands, I'd recommend checking out Amsterdam but also spending some time exploring the countryside. Maybe you can plan a long bike ride and see some highland cows!
