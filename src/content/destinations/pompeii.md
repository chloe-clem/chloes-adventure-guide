Pompeii was one of those places I remember learning about in school that just really stuck with me. The thought of a civilization being frozen in time due to the preservative nature of volcanic ash just seemed like the most fascinating thing ever.

Visiting it in person exceeded my expectations and has been one of the coolest trips history-wise that I've done so far.

I went at the perfect time of year which was in the fall. There were no crowds, so it was super easy to walk around the ruins and get some really cool pics. Also, I couldn't imagine walking around that much in the insane summer heat. You can spend a whole day in Pompeii and still not see it all, so going when the weather was nice made that so much easier! 

I highly recommend doing the guided tour. It is still a cool experience without it, but the guides know so many things about how the city worked and what everything used to be. If you do the tour, it only takes a couple of hours and then you can spend the rest of the day exploring on your own. 

I left amazed by how advanced their technologies were. Walking through the city, you can really get a sense of how lively and progressive it was. I got to see their gyms, bath houses, theater, fast food (yes you heard that right!), and so many other things. You could even see their sewage system and piping that was still built into the walls which would run water up to the rich houses.
