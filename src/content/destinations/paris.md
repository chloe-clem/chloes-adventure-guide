Despite a couple sticky situations, surprisingly Paris was such a fun experience.

My trip started bright and early at 4am after taking a 10+ hour FlixBus from Amsterdam in true budget travel fashion. I was cold, tired, and everything was closed. After camping out in a sketchy train station for a couple hours, I rallied and embarked on my big city adventure.

After doing some cool activities, the sun began to set and I started to see that the cliché is really true. The Eiffel Tower sparkling at night is pure magic. During the day, it's hard to tell why, but at night you really feel the magic of the city and can understand why so many people love it. Despite my rough start, I was so glad I came to see this.

At night, you can feel the energy of everyone being out, walking by the river, over the bridges, and enjoying dinner at rooftop bars. There's more of a calm in the air than the rush you feel during the day.

The metro was quite the experience. I'd never seen so many people crammed in such a small place, especially during the afternoon rush. It still makes me laugh to this day. I can still picture this girl's face smooshed up against the glass window of a metro car — probably the third one that passed by that we couldn't fit on!

I am not a big city person, so to me Paris is a place to visit once for a couple days but not somewhere I'd intentionally plan to go back. I am still so glad I went, and love the memories I have there.
