If trips could win awards, I think this one would get 1st place for most random. Sometimes those are the best trips, though. 

La Hulpe will always make for an excellent story among my friends and me, who picked it for a peaceful stay in a little Belgian town outside of Brussels.

The story begins when we arrived out of the 2 platform train station and started lugging our suitcases up a steep hill to our Airbnb. I think we clearly stuck out as the only tourists in town, as I suddenly saw a car pull up beside us and heard the driver ask, "are you Chloe?". To our surprise it was our Airbnb hosts who kindly offered us a ride! They set up our room so nicely, having the kitchen stocked with homemade bread and jam. We immediately felt at home.

We only stayed 1 night and had some time to kill in town before heading out to our next stop. We ventured by foot to the Château de La Hulpe which was surrounded by ponds, open fields, horses, and kilometers of forest trails. I felt like I was in a fairy tale. The best part? No one was there except the infrequent local going on their daily jog. We spent hours exploring the misty, bright green landscapes and enjoying each other's company. La Hulpe became our special find!

As for the town itself, you could probably walk across the whole main street in 10 minutes. I bet it's a wonderful place to live though, with a population of only 7,300.
