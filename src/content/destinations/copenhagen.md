I remember driving into Copenhagen for the first time and being so excited by the energy I could feel the city giving off. It was the first stop on my way to start a new study abroad semester in southern Sweden, and it immediately put me at ease knowing I would be near a big city I already was loving. Seeing bikers everywhere and the tall, skinny buildings reminded me so much of the Netherlands, which was a great sign. 

After a first walk around, I was so impressed by the style. Everyone was dressed up extremely nice and people of all ages were out walking or biking around. I also remember feeling super safe and energized by the dynamics (which is huge for me to say given that big cities usually make me feel overstimulated). 

Copenhagen had the fast-paced energy but in an organized way which made it enjoyable. I think the best way to describe it is that the city and the people in it felt extremely cool. Way cooler than me haha, but we're not here to compare.

My mom and I spent the afternoon popping in and out of thrift stores, shopping for cute decorations for my new place. I realized how much amazing shopping Copenhagen has both for home decor and clothing. It was an excellent first day. We stayed right off of the canal which made for an excellent evening walk. I'm sure it depends on the area, but in Nørrevold we felt very comfortable walking around at night. 

If I can stress one thing, its that I really love Copenhagen. I think there's some places that you would never want to live but love to travel to and there's others that make you think "yeah, this is a place I think I could really enjoy living". Copenhagen is the latter for me. I can't say there is necessarily a one must do or have to see thing like Paris with the Eiffel Tower or Athens with the Parthenon, but I love the daily life it offers. The people just seem so cool, the pastries are wonderful, the architecture is very cute, the quality of life is very high, their interior design is beautiful, and they have a great biking culture. And I could keep going on. The only thing to be aware of is it is a very very expensive city. You will be paying an outrageous amount for a meal, coffee, or pastry, but in my opinion, it is totally worth it.
