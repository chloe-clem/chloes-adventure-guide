Saint-Tropez was a very unique experience. I don't think I've ever vacationed somewhere with so much money. It made for quite the people watching. 

Walking through the winding streets of the downtown area, I admired all of the gorgeous people wearing the most beautiful outfits. 

There was also a ton of shopping and fun restaurants which brought the town to life. The best part was walking along the dock and seeing all of the yachts. I even saw one with a helicopter landing on it! People would gather here to watch the sunset and take an evening swim off the dock, which was my favorite experience.

Our post swim walk through the city, hair sopping wet and in our towels, definitely made us stand out among the crowd in their designer outfits ready to go out on the town. My friend and I still laugh about how silly we must have looked!

While I definitely didn't feel like I fit in, it was so much fun to be among the people and get a glimpse into their life. In other words, visit St Tropez if you want to see how celebrities vacation.
