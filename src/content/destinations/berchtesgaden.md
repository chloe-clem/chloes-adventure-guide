Right at the border of Germany and Austria sits Berchtesgaden National Park, and I wish I could capture in words how beautiful it is. I urge you to go see it for yourself one day.

This trip sticks out so vividly in my mind because it was one of those times I felt so alive and on top of the world. How did a girl from Texas, on a random day in December, end up across the world hiking through the most magical, snowy woods with two of her best friends? Definitely a pinch-me moment!

We actually spent 2 days here as separate day trips from Salzburg because we couldn't get enough! One of the days we took the bus to Königssee and explored through the many trails that surrounded the lake. At sunset, we took a relaxing ferry ride across the lake that brought us to even more outstanding views of the mountain range.

The second day we were more on the outskirts of Berchtesgaden National Park in Hintersee and also found some epic hikes. The highlight was the frozen lake that was filled with families ice skating, kids playing hockey, and even some brave souls cold plunging! At the top of one of our hikes, we stopped for a sandwich break and saw some paragliders taking flight off of the edge! Surprisingly the snowy paths were still hikable and many people were out exploring. It was true bliss being so connected to nature.
